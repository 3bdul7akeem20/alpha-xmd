Support & FAQ

Need help?

How do I link my bot? ▼
Enter your number on the Deploy page, get the code, open WhatsApp > Linked Devices > Link with Phone Number, and enter the code.
How do I disconnect? ▼
Enter your number on the Deploy page, click "Disconnect Session". This unlinks the bot and deletes the session.
Is my data secure? ▼
Yes. Your session is stored securely on our private servers. We do not access your chats.
